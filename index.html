For the team to decide

  • Are these six questions the right ones, in the right order?
  • Should the pilgrim see the result screen, or only the volunteer?
  • What should the QR code carry, and who scans it?
  • Which risk rules do the medical members sign off on?

What is simulated

  • Pressure: 8 zones (4 per foot) from preset scenarios, not the ESP32 yet.
  • Thermal and camera scans: placeholders only.
  • Volunteer list: invented sample screenings, no real people.

How the risk is decided

Illustrative rules, not clinically validated: an open wound, or diabetes with numbness, means high risk and a referral. A zone at 80% or more of peak load, or a 30-point left/right gap in the same zone, raises the risk to moderate. With diabetes, a high-load zone becomes high risk.
